The latest enhancements

From the headline number to the underlying trend.

Each app now combines Vizro-generated interactive charts with shared period and city filters. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days, or explore the full dataset with Last 12 months.

01

Sales & revenue

Compare confirmed revenue with sales count. Hover for detail, zoom into a period and export a chart image for your next review.

02

Interactive KPI cards

Confirmed revenue, confirmed sales, collections and average sale value update with your selected period and market. Select a card to jump to a related chart.

03

Revenue outlook

View actual revenue alongside a 14-day baseline projection based on the trailing average. Sparse data displays a clear insufficient-history message.

04

Revenue by region

A treemap reveals the contribution of each city-level market. Select a region to filter the workspace, then reset to compare all regions.

Forecasts are illustrative projections, not guarantees. Shading shows historical variability, not a calibrated confidence interval. The demos use fictitious records and do not represent live customer activity.
